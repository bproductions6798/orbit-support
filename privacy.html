Privacy Policy

Orbit: Daily Routine · Effective November 1, 2026

Orbit does not collect, sell or share your personal information.

What the app stores

The tasks, reminders, settings and progress you enter are stored on your device. Orbit has no servers that receive this data, and it does not send your data to us or to any third party.

iCloud

If you turn on iCloud sync, your Orbit data is stored in your own iCloud account so it can sync between your devices. It is managed by Apple under your Apple ID, and we cannot see it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Orbit schedules reminders on your device. They are local notifications and are not sent through any server.

No tracking

Orbit has no accounts, advertising, analytics or third-party tracking, and it does not use your data to identify you.

Children

Orbit does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
