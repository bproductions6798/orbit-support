Common questions

I'm not getting reminders.

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap Notifications, then Orbit, and make sure notifications are allowed. In Orbit, check that notifications are turned on in its own Settings. Reminders are scheduled when you open the app, so open Orbit once after changing a task.

How does iCloud sync work?

If you turn on iCloud sync in Orbit's Settings, your routine is stored in your own iCloud account and syncs between your devices signed in with the same Apple ID. It is never sent to us.

What does Strict mode do?

Strict mode is optional and off by default. When it is on, a timed task that is still not done after its snooze or grace period is locked for the day. Orbit explains the details before you turn it on.

How do I take a day off?

On the main screen, tap "Take today off." You get one day off per month, plus bonus days you earn with long perfect streaks. A day off keeps your streak safe.

How do I edit or remove a task?

Swipe a task to the right, or press and hold it, to open its editor.

How do I start over or delete my data?

Use Reset in Orbit's Settings to clear everything on the device. Deleting the app also removes its local data. If you used iCloud sync, you can manage Orbit's iCloud data from your iPhone's iCloud storage settings.

Is my data private?

Yes. Orbit has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
